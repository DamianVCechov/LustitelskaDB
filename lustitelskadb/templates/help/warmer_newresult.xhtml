How to properly upload screenshots from the game

To make your results clear and easy to compare with other players, please pay attention to the following guidelines when taking screenshots:

  • Words revealed: Upload only screenshots that clearly show the revealed words from the game.
  • Maximum number of words: Add at least one screenshot where you have revealed as many words as possible (ideally the entire completed game).
  • Visible number of guesses: Make sure that the number of attempts/guesses indicator is visible on the screenshot.
  • Up to 10 screenshots: You can attach up to 10 screenshots to one played round (e.g. the progress of the game bit by bit).

Why save screenshots

All uploaded screenshots will remain securely stored in your profile. You can return to them at any time, view the history, check the actions of others and compare which strategy each person chose when guessing.
